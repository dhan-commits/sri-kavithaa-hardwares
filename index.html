Meet Our Founder – Mr. Jayamugundhan Subramaniyam is the driving force behind Sri Kavitha Hardwares, a trusted name in hardware and construction materials since 2000. With a Diploma in Electrical and Electronics Engineering and a B.Tech in Civil Engineering, he brings valuable technical knowledge and an understanding of construction needs to the business. As a proud BNI (Business Network International) member, he believes in the power of professional networking, building meaningful business relationships, and creating opportunities through mutual trust and collaboration. Guided by a commitment to quality, integrity, and customer satisfaction, he strives to provide reliable products and dependable service to every customer. His vision goes beyond supplying construction materials—it is about helping people build stronger foundations, lasting structures, and brighter futures.
